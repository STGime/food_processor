# One Curry Base, Five Different Meals

Make a big batch of this versatile curry base on Sunday and transform it into five completely different dinners throughout the week. Less cooking, zero boredom.

## The Master Curry Base

This recipe makes enough for 5 meals. It takes about 30 minutes and stores beautifully in the fridge for up to 5 days.

**Ingredients:**
- 3 tbsp coconut oil or vegetable oil
- 3 large onions, diced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 inches fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp curry powder
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 cans (800ml) coconut milk
- 2 cans (800g) diced tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- Salt to taste

**Method:**
1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat
2. Cook onions until soft and golden, about 8 minutes
3. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 minutes until fragrant
4. Add all spices, stir for 1 minute until aromatic
5. Pour in coconut milk and diced tomatoes
6. Add tomato paste and salt
7. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally
8. Let cool and divide into 5 portions (about 1.5 cups each)

---

## Monday: Classic Chicken Curry

**Add to one portion of base:**
- 2 chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- Handful of spinach
- Serve over basmati rice

Brown the chicken in a pan, add the curry base, simmer 15 minutes until chicken is cooked through. Stir in spinach at the end. Total time: 20 minutes.

---

## Tuesday: Chickpea & Sweet Potato Curry (Vegan)

**Add to one portion of base:**
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 1 medium sweet potato, cubed small
- Fresh cilantro

Add sweet potato to the base and simmer until tender (about 12 minutes). Add chickpeas and heat through. Top with cilantro. Serve with naan bread. Total time: 18 minutes.

---

## Wednesday: Shrimp Coconut Curry

**Add to one portion of base:**
- 200g large shrimp, peeled
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- Juice of 1 lime
- Thai basil or regular basil

Sauté bell pepper for 2 minutes. Add the curry base and bring to a simmer. Add shrimp and cook 3-4 minutes until pink. Squeeze lime juice over the top. Serve over jasmine rice. Total time: 12 minutes.

---

## Thursday: Curry Lentil Soup

**Add to one portion of base:**
- 1 cup red lentils
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- Squeeze of lemon
- Crusty bread for serving

Combine base with lentils and broth. Simmer for 15-20 minutes until lentils are soft and broken down. The lentils will thicken it into a soup naturally. Finish with lemon juice. Total time: 20 minutes.

---

## Friday: Curry Noodle Bowl

**Add to one portion of base:**
- 200g rice noodles
- 1 cup shredded cabbage or bok choy
- 1 soft-boiled egg
- Sriracha and lime wedges

Cook rice noodles separately. Heat the curry base, add shredded vegetables and cook 3 minutes. Ladle over noodles. Top with a halved soft-boiled egg, sriracha, and a lime wedge. Total time: 15 minutes.

---

## Meal Prep Tips

- **Storage:** Keep portions in airtight containers in the fridge. The flavors actually improve after a day.
- **Freezing:** The base freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge.
- **Scaling:** Double the base recipe and freeze half for the following week.
- **Spice level:** Add chili flakes, fresh chilies, or chili paste to individual portions to customize heat levels day by day.

---

## Why This Works

The base does all the heavy lifting — aromatics, spices, and the coconut-tomato sauce are where 90% of the flavor lives. The daily add-ins just provide protein, texture, and variety. You're essentially cooking once and eating five very different meals.
