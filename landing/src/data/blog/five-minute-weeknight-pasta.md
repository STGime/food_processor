# 5 Weeknight Pastas You Can Make in Under 20 Minutes

Quick doesn't mean boring. These five pasta recipes use pantry staples and come together faster than delivery. Each one is designed for those nights when you're tired, hungry, and don't want to think too hard.

## 1. Cacio e Pepe

The Roman classic that needs just three ingredients.

**Ingredients:**
- 400g spaghetti or tonnarelli
- 200g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
- 2 tsp freshly cracked black pepper

**Method:**
Cook pasta in well-salted water. While it cooks, toast the black pepper in a dry pan for 30 seconds. Add a ladle of pasta water to the pepper. When the pasta is al dente, transfer it directly to the pan. Remove from heat, add the cheese in batches, tossing vigorously and adding splashes of pasta water until you get a creamy sauce. Serve immediately.

**Tip:** The key is working off the heat. Too much heat and the cheese clumps instead of emulsifying.

## 2. Garlic Butter Pasta with Lemon

Bright, buttery, and ready in 15 minutes.

**Ingredients:**
- 400g linguine
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- Red pepper flakes
- Parmesan, grated
- Fresh parsley

**Method:**
Cook pasta. In a large pan, melt butter over medium heat. Add sliced garlic and cook until just golden (about 2 minutes — watch it closely). Add red pepper flakes and lemon zest. Toss in the drained pasta with a cup of pasta water. Squeeze in the lemon juice, top with Parmesan and parsley.

## 3. Midnight Aglio e Olio

The pasta you make at midnight when nothing else will do.

**Ingredients:**
- 400g spaghetti
- 6 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt

**Method:**
Cook pasta very al dente. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add garlic slices and red pepper flakes, cooking slowly until the garlic is golden and fragrant (3-4 minutes). Transfer pasta to the pan with a good amount of pasta water. Toss over heat until the sauce comes together. Finish with parsley and a drizzle of raw olive oil.

## 4. Creamy Tomato Vodka Penne

Restaurant-quality in under 20 minutes.

**Ingredients:**
- 400g penne rigate
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 400g can crushed San Marzano tomatoes
- 60ml vodka
- 120ml heavy cream
- Parmesan, grated
- Fresh basil

**Method:**
Cook penne. Sauté garlic in olive oil for 1 minute. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 8 minutes. Add vodka and cook for 2 minutes to burn off the alcohol. Stir in cream and simmer for another 2 minutes. Toss in the pasta, add Parmesan and pasta water as needed. Top with torn basil.

**Tip:** The vodka isn't for flavor — it releases aromatic compounds in the tomatoes that aren't water or fat soluble.

## 5. Brown Butter and Sage Orecchiette

Nutty, herby, and impossibly simple.

**Ingredients:**
- 400g orecchiette
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter
- 12-15 fresh sage leaves
- Parmesan, grated
- Salt and black pepper

**Method:**
Cook orecchiette. In a large pan, melt butter over medium heat and cook until it turns golden brown and smells nutty (about 3-4 minutes). Add sage leaves and fry until crispy (30 seconds). Toss in drained pasta with a splash of pasta water. Season with salt, pepper, and plenty of Parmesan.

## The Pattern

Notice something? Every recipe here follows the same formula: **cook pasta + build a simple sauce in the time it takes to boil**. Once you internalize this pattern, you can improvise endlessly. That's weeknight cooking at its best.
